UDDI registry provide aims at providing information regarding business and its services and make it easy for service consumer. UDDI Registry is one by which web services list themselves on the internet. Universal Description discovery and Integration is a platform independent and open framework. UDDI Version 2.0 was published in june 2001. UDDI registry was developed to register any service of web services.

The elements that are associated with UDDI business registry
  • Yellow Page
  • White Page

White Page: White Page are the Informations that consists of contact number, contact name and phone number are the yellow pages.

Yellow Page: Yellow pages provide a classification of the service or business. The taxonomy under which the concept is categories. Taxonomy is the way it organizes its data into categories and subcategories.
