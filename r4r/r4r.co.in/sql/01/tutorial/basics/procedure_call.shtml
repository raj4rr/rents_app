How to Call One Procedure From Other

  • It is possible to execute and call one  procedure from within another procedure
  • A procedure that calls or executes another procedure is known as the calling procedure
  • A procedure that is called or executed by the calling procedure is termed as the called procedure

For Example

CREATE PROCEDURE  infList @name char(15)
AS
BEGIN
EXEC  addressList @name
EXEC  empNameList
END
SELECT  emp_id,emp_name,emp_add
FROM  employee
WHERE  emp_name=@name
