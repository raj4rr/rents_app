In this tutorial I am going to teach you how to convert a picture to a sketch picture, I have a color caw picture to convert a sketch. Let's see how did it.

Step 1: Take any color picture to modify, I chose here caw picture.

Step 2: Go to Image menu -> Adjustment -> Desaturate to make black and white picture.

Step 3: Go to Filter menu -> Other -> High Pass option and set radius as here.

You should have same picture.

Step 4: Now make sketch effect, go to Image menu -> Adjustment -> Equalize that picture.
