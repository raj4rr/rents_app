Make Sky Reflection On Car
Previous Home Next

You are going to learn a simple way to make a reflection effect on any type of glass, I have chosen to make reflection effect on the glass of the car.

Step 1: Take two picture to modify, I have taken here a car our clouds picture to modify.

Step 2: Select Polygonal Lasso tool (L key) and make selection of the glass to make reflection.

Step3: Copy sky picture and go to Edit menu -> Past into, the picture would be as here.

Step 4: Now reduce the layer opacity 100% to 85%, the sky reflection picture is competed.
