In this example I am going to convert a picture to a old picture look, I have a boat picture to modify. You can also make that by following this example.

Step 1: Take any type of picture to modify, I have chose a boat picture for that.

Step 2: Now make duplicate layer of the picture and reduce opacity then change blending mode as given here.

The picture would be same.

Step 3: Color settings, go to Image menu -> Adjustment -> Hue/Saturation and make settings of the color.

Step 4: Photo filter effect, go to Image menu -> Adjustment -> Photo Filter and make settings as given here.
