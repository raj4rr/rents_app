It is so easy to change the human eye color, I have try to do that so lets start.

Step 1: Take a eye picture as I have taken here.

Step 2: Make selection by the polygonal lasso tool (L key), you can also make selection by Pen Tool (P key) its depend on your wish.

Step 3: Make some color setting, Go to Image menu -> Adjustments -> Curves
