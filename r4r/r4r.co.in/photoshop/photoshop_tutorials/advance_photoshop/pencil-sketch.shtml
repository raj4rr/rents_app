In this example we will teach you how to convert a picture into a a pencil sketch picture, I have selected a boy picture to modify. Follow this example and make this effect.

Step 1: Take a picture to convert a pencil effect, and press Ctrl + Shift + U key to make black and white picture.

Step 2: Give paint daubs effect, go to Filter menu -> Artistic -> Paint Daubs and set value as here.

Step 3: Now give blur effect, go to Filter menu -> Blur -> Gaussian blur and set radius.

The picture should be same.

Step 4: Change the layer blending mode as here.
