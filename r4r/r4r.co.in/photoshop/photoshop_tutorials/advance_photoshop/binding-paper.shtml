This example will help you to convert two page into as binding paper, I have taken here two blank page to modify. Follow this example to learn completely.

Step 1: Take any type of two page as I have here.

Step 2: Select Brush tool (B key) and set size as given here.

Step 3: Now make black spot in between two line of the page as here.

Step 4: Select brush tool again and reduce size "6" to "2".

Step 5: Draw a line to combine two black spot as here.

Step 6: Make bevel and emboss effect, go to layer menu > layer style > bevel and emboss and make settings.
