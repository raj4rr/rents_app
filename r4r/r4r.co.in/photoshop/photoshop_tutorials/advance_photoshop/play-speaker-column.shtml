Animated Music Speaker Column

In this example I will teach you how to make animated music speaker column, I have a still stop speaker picture to make active like playing music so follow this example to make this effect.

Step 1: Take a new picture of the column as here.

Step 2: Now make selection by the Pen tool ( P key) of the speaker cover as here.

Step 3: Make copy and past on the new layer then give blur effect, go to Filter menu -> Blur -> Gaussian blur and set radius as here.

Step 4: Now press right arrow key to move right side.

Step 5: Make duplicate layer and press left arrow key two time.

Step 6: Now set speaker layer separate frame as here.
