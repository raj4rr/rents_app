Flowing Chimney Fumes
Previous Home Next

In this example you will learn how to make an animated fumes on the chimney, you need a chimney picture to make this effect so follow now to learn completely.

Step 1: Take a chimney picture to modify.

Step 2: Take a new layer and brush tool (B key) with white color and draw on the face of the chimney.

Step 3: Make selection of the fumes and delete unwanted part.

Step 4: Take burn tool and apply in the fumes as here.

Step 5: Give Gaussian blur effect, go to Filter menu -> Blur -> Gaussian blur and set radius.

The picture will be look like this.

Step 6: Take a new layer and increase fumes by the Smudge tool (R key) as here.

Step 7: Use same step to make one two more layer as done up.

Step 8: Now make animation, go to Window menu -> Animation and set every layer on separate frame as given here.
