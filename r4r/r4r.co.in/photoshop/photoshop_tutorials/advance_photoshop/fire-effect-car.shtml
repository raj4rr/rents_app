Fire On the Car
Previous Home Next

In this example you will learn how to make fire effect on any object, I have selected a racing car to make fire effect. follow to learn yourself and enjoy.

Step 1: Take any vehicle picture to modify as here.

Step 2: Make selection of the tyre and change foreground and background color then fill in the selection.

Step 3: Add noise, go to Filter menu -> Noise -> add noise and make settings as given here.

The picture should be same.

Step 4: Give wind effect, go to Filter menu -> Stylize -> Wind make selection of the option as here.

The picture will look like this.

Step 5: Give ripple effect, go to Filter menu -> Distort and make settings.

The picture will look like this.

Step 6: Make color setting, go to Image menu -> Adjustment -> Hue/Saturation and set values as here.

Step 7: Now give contrast and brightness, go to Image menu -> Adjustment -> Brightness/Contrast and set values as here.

The picture should be same.

Step 8: Now change blending mode of the layer "Normal" to "Linear Dodge".

Final picture
