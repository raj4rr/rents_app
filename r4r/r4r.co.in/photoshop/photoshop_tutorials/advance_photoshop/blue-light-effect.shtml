In this tutorial you will learn how to make a blue light effect on any picture, I have selected a beautiful picture to make this effect, all the steps are necessary to make this effect as given in this example

Step 1: Take a picture to make this effect as here.

Step 2: Select foreground color "#15347a" and take a new layer then fill foreground color in the new layer.

Step 3: Now make setting of the blending mode "Normal" to "Hard Light" the picture will look like this.

Step 4: Give lighting effect, go to Filter menu > Render > Lighting effect, and make settings as here.
