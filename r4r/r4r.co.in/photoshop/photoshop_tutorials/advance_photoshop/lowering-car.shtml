In this example you will learn how to make a lowering car effect, you need a car picture to make this effect follow to know learn and make yourself.

Step 1: Take a car picture to make this effect, I have selected a honda car as here.

Step 2: Take Polygonal lasso tool (L key) our Pen tool (P key) to make selection bottom par of the car with tyre as I have done here.

Step 3: Press Ctrl + X to cut the selected part, take a new layer and past then pull down this layer from the car upper layer as given in the layer.

Step 4: Now select move tool (V key) and move up tyre picture
