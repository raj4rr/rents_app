Negative Picture Effect
Previous Home Next

In this example you will learn how to convert a colored picture into negative picture effect, I have a beautiful lady picture. Follow this example to make this effect on any picture.

Step 1: Take any picture to modify I have selected a beautiful girl picture.

Step 2: Make black and white photo effect, go to Image menu -> Adjustment -> Desaturate.

Step 3: Now press Ctrl + I to make invert picture as given here.

Step 4: Change color settings, go to Image menu -> Adjustment -> Level and set values.
