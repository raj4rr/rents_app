If you are looking to make bright any photograph of the person our any place, you should follow this tutorial completely to make bright picture.

Step 1: Take picture to modify, I have a boy photograph to modify.

Step 2: Increase the level of the photo, go to Image menu -> Adjustment -> level and make same settings.

Step 3: Now Make brighness, go to Image menu -> Adjustment -> Brightness/Contrast and make same settings.

Step 4: Change the color settings, go to Image menu -> Adjustment -> Color Balance and make same settings.

Your bright picture is completed
