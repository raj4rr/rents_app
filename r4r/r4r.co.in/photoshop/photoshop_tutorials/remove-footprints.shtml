Remove Foot Prints On The Sand Picture

In this example, you can learn how to remove any type of object from the document, I going to remove footprint on the sand picture so follow this example carefully.

Step 1: Take any picture to modify, here is a sand picture to modify.

Step 2: Select Spot Healing Brush tool (J key) and make settings as given below.

Step 3: Hold Ctrl + mouse click around the object which you want to remove as here.

Step 4: Now move mouse footprint after moved mouse, you see the invisible footpring.

Step 5: Use same step to remove another footprit.

This is your removed footprints picture.
