Make a Bitten in Apple
Previous Home Next

In this example, you will learn how to make a bitten effect on the apple. I have done it with some steps those are given in this example, follow all the step to make that.

Step 1: Take a apple picture to modify.

Step 2: Select Polygonal lasso tool (L key) and make selection on the apple as here.

Step 3: Take a new layer and fill '#f7eabd' color in the selection by the Paint Bucket tool (G key).

Step 4: Give Shadow effect, go to layer menu -> layer style -> inner shadow and make settings.

Step 5: Give stain effect, go to layer menu -> layer style -> Stain and make settings.
