Enhancement Eye

In this example you will learn how to enhance the eye, you can choose any person eye, I have taken here a woman eye to give enhance effect.

Step 1: Take any eye picture from you collection to modify as looking here.

Step 2: Select Polygonal lasso tool (L key) and make selection of the whole eye.

Step 3: Use level effect, go to Image menu -> Adjustment -> Level and make same setting to give bright effect.

As here

Step 4: Now Select eyeball by the same selection tool as used up.

Step 5: Use brightness and contrast to make real enhancing, go to Image menu -> Adjustment -> Brightness and Contrast and make setting.

Final real picture
