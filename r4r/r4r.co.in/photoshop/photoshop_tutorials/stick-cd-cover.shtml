You are going to learn how to stick a company cover on the back of the Compact Disc by this example, you will enjoy it during study this example.

Step 1: Take two photo 'CD' and 'Cover of the company', I have taken a photo related chemistry.

Step 2: Now hold Ctrl key and mouse click on the CD picture layer to make selection as here.

Step 3: Make copy of the company cover picture as I have chemistry photo and go to file menu -> and choose past into. The picture should be same as here.

Step 4: Drop shadow effect, go to layer menu -> layer style -> Drop shadow and make settings.
