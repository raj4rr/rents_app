In this example, you will learn how to make yellow teeth to white teeth, I have a boy teeth picture to modify the him teeth so follow how I am doing that.

Step 1: Take any teeth picture as you like, I have taken here a boy teeth picture.

Step 2: Select Polygonal lasso tool (L key) and make selection of the teeth as here.

Step 3: Now go to Image menu -> Adjustment -> Desaturate, the picture should be same.

Step 4: Give Brightness, go to Image menu -> Adjustment and make setting of the Brightness/contrast.

The white teeth picture is completed.
