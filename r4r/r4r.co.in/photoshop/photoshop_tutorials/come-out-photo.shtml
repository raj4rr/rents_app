Come Out From the Photo

This is very interesting tutorial because you will learn how to make out of any person our another things from the photograph, I have taken here a boy photo who is jumping in the photograph and try to make him out of the photograph during dump.

Step 1: First take any person photograph as I have taken here then select Rectangular marquee tool (M key) and make selection as here.

Step 2: Now cover boy under selection, select Polygonal lasso tool (L key) and Click with shift key to make selection.

Step 3: Delete upper selected area, after selection press shift + I key and press del key to delete the selected area.

This picture should be as here.

Step 4: Increase file size, go to Image menu -> Canvas Size and use same settings as here.

Step 5: Make again selection of the background by the Rectangular marquee tool (M key) as done up.

Step 6: Go to Select menu -> Modify -> border and set width '4' px then take a new layer and fill color as you like. I have filled here black color.
