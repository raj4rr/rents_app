In this example, I will teach you how to turn any picture our paper corner in any shape. I have a flower picture to make turn paper corner effect, let try.

Step 1: Take any picture you want to modify as here.

Step 2: Select Polygonal Lasso tool (L key) to make selection of the picture corner as I have done here.

Step 3: Press Del key to delete the selection part of the picture.

Step 4: Use gradient Overlay effect, go to layer menu -> layer style -> gradient overlay and make same setting as given here.

Step 5: Use drop shadow effect, go to layer menu -> layer style -> drop shadow and make same settings.

Your turn picture corner picture is completed.
