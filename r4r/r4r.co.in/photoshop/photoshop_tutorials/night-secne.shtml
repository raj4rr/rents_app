In this example you are going to lean how to draw star and add star in the sky at night time, I am sure you will be able to draw that by follow this example.

Step 1: Take any night scene picture as I have take here a city picture.

Step 2: Select Pen tool (P key) and draw with white color as here.

Step 3: Make duplicate layer and press Ctrl + T key and right click on the selection then choose as here.

Step 4: Make duplicate layer and press Ctrl + T key and adjust as here.

Step 5: Now make duplicate layer again and press Ctrl + T key and right click then choose as here.

You star is completed.
