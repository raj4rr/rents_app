Convert a Black Picture To Bright Picture

This is very interesting tutorial, I am sure you will like it. In this example you will learn how to make black picture to bright. I have a picture to modify so follow now.

Step 1: Take any picture to make bright, I have a boy picture to modify.

Step 2: Select Edit in quick mask mode (Q key) and move the mouse on the area that would be modify as I have done here.

Step 3: Now Select Edit in Standard Mode tool, the picture would be convert into selection picture as here.

Step 4: Increase level settings, go to Image menu -> Adjustment -> level and make settings.

Step 5: Do color balance, go to Image menu -> Adjustment -> color balance and make settings.

Step 6: Increase Brightness and contrast settings, go to Image menu -> Adjustment -> Brightness/contrast and make settings.

Step 7: Make canvas settings, go to Image menu -> Adjustment -> Canvas and make settings as I have done here.
