Gameplay Rules

  • Prepare grass with Hoe (1).
  • Pick a seed card and plant with Seed (2).
  • Water crops with Water (3) based on each seed capacity.
  • Harvest mature crops with Harvest (4).
  • Reach each level goal before season days run out.
  • Press B to buy seeds, N for next level, R to restart run.
